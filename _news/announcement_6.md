New working paper draft completed: *When Stablecoins Meet Treasury Scarcity — Global Spillovers from Dollar Settlement*. The paper develops a two-country DSGE model of how USD-stablecoin settlement, backed by a scarce supply of short-term US Treasuries, opens a new channel of international macroeconomic spillovers.
